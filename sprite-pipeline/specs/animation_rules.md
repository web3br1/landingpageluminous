# Animation Rules

## General Rules

- Maintain consistent anatomy across all frames.
- Maintain consistent camera angle across all states.
- Maintain consistent palette and outline thickness.
- Keep motion readable with anticipation, impact, and recovery poses where possible.
- Prioritize gameplay readability over animation complexity.

## Frame Counts

Default frame count per state: 6 frames.

Allowed exceptions:

- Hurt may use 3–4 distinct poses repeated into the row.
- Sleep may use 3–4 subtle poses repeated into the row.
- Knockout may use a short fall/settle sequence repeated or padded.

## Combat Readability

- Physical attack must show body mechanics and impact direction.
- Special attack must show the character as the source of the effect.
- Movement must read as tactical board movement, not a cinematic run cycle with shifting perspective.

## Consistency

Every frame must feel like the same character model. Do not let generated frames mutate proportions, facial features, accessories, or limbs.
