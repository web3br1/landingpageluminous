# Animation States Prompt Reference

Every character sprite sheet must include the following states.

## 1. Idle

Neutral combat-ready stance with breathing or subtle energy. Must clearly show the character’s default silhouette.

## 2. Movement

Readable tactical movement loop. Keep the body grounded and the direction consistent with the game camera.

## 3. Physical Attack

Close-range strike, bite, slash, tackle, claw, punch, horn, tail, or body-impact action. The attack arc must be readable at small scale.

## 4. Special Attack

Elemental, magical, ranged, aura, psychic, fire, water, nature, electric, or signature effect. Effects must support the sprite without overwhelming it.

## 5. Hurt

Clear recoil, compressed posture, or impact response while preserving silhouette recognition.

## 6. Sleep

Resting or drowsy state. The character must remain identifiable and should not use background props.

## 7. Knockout / Defeat

Defeated state suitable for a tactical battle board. Avoid gore, realism, or overly dramatic collapse.

## 8. Victory Pose

Confident post-battle expression or gesture that reinforces personality without changing the character model.
