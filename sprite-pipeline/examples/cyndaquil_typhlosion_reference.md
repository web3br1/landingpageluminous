# Example — Cyndaquil / Typhlosion-Inspired Fire Monster Reference

## Character Interpretation

A compact fire-type monster with a rounded mammalian body, short limbs, closed or determined eyes, and a prominent flame identity. The sprite should preserve the recognizable fire-backed silhouette and energetic battle personality while translating it into the fixed tactical monster battle atlas style.

## Main Prompt Seed

Create a production-ready tactical 3/4 pixel-art sprite atlas for a compact fire monster inspired by a rounded quilled creature evolving toward a fierce flame-backed battler. Preserve the rounded snout, compact body mass, short limbs, dark upper body accents, cream lower body, and iconic back flame silhouette. Render it as a Nintendo DS-era tactical RPG monster battle sprite sheet with clean dark outline, compact indexed palette, transparent background, and one animation state per row.

## Notes

- Keep flames readable but not over-rendered.
- Do not make the sprite semi-realistic.
- Do not let flames obscure the body silhouette.
- Preserve the same body proportions in every frame.
